## 🔒 Risk Mitigation

### Market Risks
- **Competition:** Differentiate via UX & features
- **Market Saturation:** Focus on underserved regions
- **Regulation Changes:** Monitor policy, adapt quickly

### Technical Risks
- **Scaling Issues:** Invest in load testing, auto-scaling
- **Security Breaches:** Regular audits, bug bounties
- **Payment Failures:** Multiple provider redundancy

### Operational Risks
- **Team Attrition:** Competitive compensation, growth opportunities
- **Cash Flow:** Conservative burn rate, revenue focus
- **Customer Support:** Hire early, build systems
